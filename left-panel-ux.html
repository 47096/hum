My Recommendation

Option 3: Hybrid

AI bar at top for quick generation. Genre/subgenre collapse into "Advanced" section. Simple default, powerful when needed. Keeps genre discovery for users who want it.

Why not Option 2?

Removing genre chips entirely loses the discovery aspect. Users who don't know what they want benefit from browsing genres. Hybrid keeps both paths open.

Why not Option 4?

Two modes adds UI complexity. Hybrid achieves the same goal with progressive disclosure — no tab switching needed.
