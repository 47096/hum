What to adopt from Suno

Adopt Worth copying

  • Per-field AI generation (our sparkle approach is better than Suno's single action)
  • Structure tags as first-class citizens
  • Style + lyrics as separate fields
  • Multiple versions per generation

Adapt Modify to fit

  • Simple mode → could add a "quick generate" that fills both fields from one prompt
  • Genre tags → we already have chips, could add more
  • Album art → could add AI-generated art later

Our edge What we do better

  • Lyrics templates (Suno doesn't have these)
  • Granular AI control per field
  • Genre/subgenre chip system
  • Draggable lyrics panel

Skip Not needed

  • Suno's "weirdness" slider (too niche)
  • Voice cloning (complex, legal issues)
  • Social features (out of scope)
