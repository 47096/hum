Song structure:
[Intro] -- short opening, set the mood
[Verse] -- tell the story, build the narrative
[Pre Chorus] -- build tension before the hook
[Chorus] -- the main hook, catchy and repeatable
[Post Chorus] -- reinforcement after the hook
[Hook] -- the most memorable part, the earworm
[Bridge] -- change of perspective or new angle
[Interlude] -- instrumental break between sections
[Transition] -- connect two sections smoothly
[Build Up] -- rising energy before a drop or chorus
[Break] -- stripped-back moment, tension release
[Inst] -- instrumental section, no vocals
[Solo] -- featured instrument performance
[Outro] -- wrap it up, fade or strong ending
