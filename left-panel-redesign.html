My Recommendation

Option 2: Top 5 + More button

Show top 5 style templates and top 5 lyrics templates by default. "More" button reveals the rest. Keeps discoverability while reducing clutter. Best balance of simplicity and power.

Why not Option 4 (Dropdowns)?

Dropdowns are compact but less discoverable. Users can't see all options at a glance. Chips are more visual and easier to scan quickly.

Why not Option 5 (Progressive)?

Progressive disclosure hides too much. Templates are useful for inspiration — hiding them behind a toggle reduces their value.

Why not Option 6 (Simplified)?

Too minimal. Templates help users who don't know what to write. Removing them removes a key feature.
